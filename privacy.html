Privacy Policy

The Startup Blog Last updated: September 12, 2026

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit boldblog.in.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you subscribe to our mailing list, we collect your email address. That's it. We don't ask for your name, company, or any other personal details.

Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, we use analytics to understand how our site is used. We collect standard technical information through our analytics provider:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring website or source (how you found us)
  • Device type, browser, and operating system
  • General geographic location (country/city level — never precise location)

We use privacy-focused analytics that do not track you across other websites, do not create a personal profile about you, and do not share data with advertising networks.

How We Use Your Information

  • Email address: Solely to send you our newsletter when we publish new content. We will never sell, rent, or share your email with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • Analytics data: To understand which content resonates with readers and improve our site. This data is aggregated and anonymized — we cannot identify individual visitors from it.

Cookies

Our site uses a minimal number of cookies:

  • Analytics cookies: Set by our analytics provider to distinguish between new and returning visitors. These are first-party cookies and do not track you elsewhere.
  • Essential cookies: If we add any functionality that requires session state (e.g., dark mode preference), we use minimal first-party cookies.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels from social media platforms, or third-party marketing cookies.

Third-Party Services

Our site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process request logs as part of their infrastructure, but we do not share personal data with them beyond what's necessary to serve the website.

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any advertising-oriented tracking tools.

Data Retention

  • Email subscribers: Your email is retained as long as you are subscribed. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email, or by contacting us directly.
  • Analytics data: Retained for up to 14 months for trend analysis, then automatically deleted.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Deletion — Request that we delete your personal data
  • Unsubscribe — Opt out of our mailing list at any time
  • Correction — Request correction of any inaccurate data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at cinechillz01@gmail.com. We respond to all legitimate requests within 14 days.

Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top. Significant changes will be noted in our newsletter.

Contact
