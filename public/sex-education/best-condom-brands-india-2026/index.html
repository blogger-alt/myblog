Q: Flavored condoms safe hain? A: Oral sex ke liye yes. Vaginal sex ke liye — kuch flavored condoms mein sugar hota hai jo infection cause kar sakta hai. Check karein ki vaginal use approved hai.

Q: Condom kahan store karein? A: Cool, dry jagah. Direct sunlight se door. Wallet mein nahi. Bedside drawer ideal hai.

Q: Expired condom use kar sakte hain? A: Nahi — expired condom break hone ki probability zyada hoti hai. Always check expiry date.
