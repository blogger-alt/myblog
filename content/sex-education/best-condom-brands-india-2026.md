## Condom Use Karne Ka Sahi Tarika

1. **Expiry date check karo** — expired condom use mat karo
2. **Packet carefully kholein** — teeth ya scissors se nahi
3. **Air bubble squeeze karo** — tip mein space rakhein
4. **Erection ke baad pehno** — pehle nahi
5. **Sahi direction mein roll karo** — andar wali taraf nahi
6. **After sex turant hatao** — carefully

**Common mistakes:**
- ❌ Dono condoms ek saath — friction se dono break hote hain
- ❌ Oil-based lube with latex — breaks the latex
- ❌ Storing in wallet — heat se damage
- ❌ Freezer mein rakhna — cold se brittle ho jaata hai

## FAQ

**Q: India mein sabse best condom kaunsa hai?**
A: Overall best Durex Invisible hai — ultra thin aur most reliable. Budget mein Skore NotOut best value hai.

**Q: Condom kitna safe hai pregnancy se bachane mein?**
A: Sahi use se 98% effective hai. Galat use se 85% effective. Pill ke saath 99%+.

**Q: Flavored condoms safe hain?**
A: Oral sex ke liye yes. Vaginal sex ke liye — kuch flavored condoms mein sugar hota hai jo infection cause kar sakta hai. Check karein ki vaginal use approved hai.

**Q: Condom kahan store karein?**
A: Cool, dry jagah. Direct sunlight se door. Wallet mein nahi. Bedside drawer ideal hai.

**Q: Expired condom use kar sakte hain?**
A: Nahi — expired condom break hone ki probability zyada hoti hai. Always check expiry date.
