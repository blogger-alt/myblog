## Premature Ejaculation Kya Hai?

Medical definition ke mutabik, agar aap sex start hone ke 1-2 minutes ke andar ejaculate karte hain aur ise control nahi kar paate — yeh premature ejaculation (PE) hai.

**Types of PE:**

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Lifelong PE | Pehle sex se hi yeh problem hai |
| Acquired PE | Pehle normal tha, baad mein problem aayi |
| Situational PE | Sirf kuch situations mein hota hai |
| Variable PE | Kabhi kabhi hota hai |

### Technique 1 — Start-Stop Method ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Yeh sabse effective technique hai — research mein 95% success rate.

## When to See a Doctor?

Doctor se milein agar:
- Techniques se koi improvement nahi aa rahi 3 months baad
- PE ki wajah se relationship problems ho rahi hain
- Pain ya discomfort hai
- Anxiety ya depression ke symptoms hain

**Medical treatments available:**
- SSRIs (dapoxetine) — prescription medicine
- Topical anesthetics — doctor prescribed
- Sex therapy — psychologist ke saath
- CBT (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy)

**India mein kahan jaayein:**
- Andrologist ya urologist — physical issues ke liye
- Sex therapist — psychological issues ke liye
- Online consultation — Mann Talks, iCall
